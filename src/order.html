How to Place an Order

  1. Choose items: Check the menu and decide what you want.
  2. Pay at the counter: Tell the cashier your order and pay.
  3. Collect your token: Keep the token until your food is ready.
  4. Pick up your food: Listen for your token number and collect your order.

Peak Hours

The cafeteria may be busiest from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM. Allow extra time.

Pickup Rules

Keep your token safe, check your order, and do not block the serving counter.
